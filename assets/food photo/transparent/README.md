# Transparent food assets

These cutouts were created with the built-in image generation tool from the original WebP photos in the parent folder. The page uses the transparent versions; the original photos remain available. PNG files are the generated originals. WebP files are smaller copies exported at quality 90 with their alpha channel preserved, for use on the page.

## Edit prompt

The following prompt was applied separately to pasta, chickandfries, steak, stirfry, cordon blu, and pho:

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied [filename] food photograph. Remove ONLY the white background OUTSIDE the complete serving plate or bowl. Output genuine transparent alpha. Preserve all original food, the full plate/bowl including its white areas, original camera angle, composition, colors and proportions exactly. Do not change, redraw, rearrange or restyle the food. Keep a subtle natural edge shadow with transparent falloff. Fit the whole object inside the canvas, with minimal transparent margin and no cropping. No added objects, text, watermark or checkerboard.

Pizza prompt:

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied pizza photograph. Remove only the plain white background outside the pizza and wooden serving board, producing a genuine transparent alpha background. Preserve the entire board, pizza, toppings, original photography, original orientation, proportions and framing exactly; do not redraw or restyle the dish. Retain a subtle natural edge shadow with transparent falloff. Keep all white food details opaque. No added objects, text or checkerboard.
